Import copies events from the calendar on your phone into Large Print Calendar, within the period and conditions the app defines. Export copies events from Large Print Calendar into the calendar on your phone.

If you uninstall the app, events already copied into another calendar stay there. You would need to delete them in that calendar app yourself.

6. Public holidays and date information

The app shows New Zealand public holidays and the regional Anniversary Day for the region you choose in Settings, as published by Employment New Zealand and the New Zealand Government (govt.nz), and the Matariki dates set in the Te Kāhui o Matariki Public Holiday Act 2022. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the official guidance gives two days (for example, a Saturday or the following Monday, depending on whether you would normally work that day); the app shows the weekday as the day off and the weekend day by name only. It does not show every local, school, or workplace holiday. Holiday dates are included for 2026 to 2028; outside that period the app shows no holiday information.

Some dates are marked as provisional because the government had not yet published them when the app was released (for example, 2028 dates other than Matariki). Holiday information comes from official sources and may differ from the real thing if a source changes or is published later. For dates that matter, please confirm with the official source as well.
