Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23

connectopia Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"), a company based in the Republic of Korea, makes Large Print Calendar ("the app") for people who want a calendar that is easy to read and easy to use. This policy explains what personal information the app handles when it is set to the Singapore region, why, and the rights you have under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (the PDPA).

We are the organisation responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about it at any time (§15).

1. At a glance

  • Your events, notes, step counts, and most app settings stay on your device. They are not sent to our servers automatically.
  • We never ask for or collect your precise location or your contacts.
  • Advertising is disabled for Singapore in the current app configuration.
  • The app does not use your events, notes, name or step values for advertising.
  • Usage and error reports help us improve the app. They are on by default, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings > Help & backup > Send diagnostic info.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You have rights over your information under the PDPA, including access, correction and withdrawal of consent. We will respond to requests within a reasonable period and explain if more time is needed. You can also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission (§10).
  • Sending us feedback from the app is optional. If you do, we keep it on our servers for one year (§4b).

2. What we handle

a. On your device

  • Profile: the name you choose to be called and your daily step goal
  • Events: title, notes, date, time, reminders, and repeat settings
  • Calendar import and export: events and calendar names from the calendar on your phone that you select (such as Google Calendar), and the result of each import or export
  • App settings: calendar display, region (including which bank holidays you follow) and language, daily reminders, step display, and vibration
  • Health and activity: step counts, your step goal, goals you reached, and whether the activity permission is granted
  • Backup files you create yourself

Your step counts stay on your device. They are not sent to our servers or to any analytics service. Whether a permission was granted, or whether a feature worked, may be recorded without any step values.

b. On our servers and in Firebase

  • Firebase anonymous authentication ID and app installation identifier
  • App integrity tokens (Play Integrity on Android; App Attest with DeviceCheck fallback on iOS) with basic device and app information
  • Consent records: that you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, the version you accepted, when you accepted it, the app's region and language, your device platform, and a local installation key
  • Server calls: which server feature was called, your IP address, the time, and whether the call succeeded
  • Remote settings: your country code, language, time zone, and operating-system and app versions, used to deliver the right app settings
  • Usage reports (Google Analytics for Firebase): which screens and features were used and how often, with the app instance identifier, basic device and app information, and your device's advertising identifier on Android when available, or on iOS only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency (ATT). They never include your name, your events, your notes, or your step values
  • Error reports (Firebase Crashlytics): crashes and non-fatal errors, stack traces, the app's state at the time, and device, operating-system and app versions
  • Feedback ("Share feedback" screen): the message you write (5 to 2,000 characters); an email address, only if you choose to enter one so that we can reply; and, only if "Include device information" is on, the app version and build number, the device language and country, the device model, and the operating-system version. We do not add your anonymous ID, installation ID or analytics ID to feedback. A copy of each message, with the same details, is also delivered to our company email inbox so we do not miss it. To limit spam, we keep for two hours a one-way hash (not the original value) of your app's integrity token, or of your IP address if the token is not available, with a count of messages sent in that hour.
  • Unsent feedback: if a message cannot be sent, the app keeps up to 20 unsent messages on your device, not encrypted, and tries again later, until they are sent or you clear the app's data.

The app does not request GPS or precise location permission. Advertising is disabled in the current Singapore configuration. If advertising is introduced, this policy and any required choice flow will be updated before it is enabled.

3. Why we handle it and the basis for doing so

  • To provide the calendar, events, reminders, backup, calendar import and export, and step features you ask for. Basis: you ask for these features and agree to the Terms of Service.
  • To keep your settings, and to record that you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy. Basis: your consent, given when you accept them.
  • To find and fix errors, keep the service stable, prevent abuse of our servers, and answer your questions. Basis: our legitimate interests in running a secure and reliable service, which are not outweighed by any adverse effect on you.
  • To understand which features are used and how the app performs, so we can improve it. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time, simply and free of charge, by turning off Settings > Help & backup > Send diagnostic info; reports stop being sent from that moment.
  • To read and answer the feedback you send, investigate problems you report (using the device information, if you included it), reply if you gave an email address, and improve the app. Basis: your consent, given when you send feedback. Giving an email address and device information is optional.

If you do not use an optional feature, or you decline a permission, the related information is not handled. Only that feature is limited.

4. How long we keep it

a. On your device

  • Profile, app settings, events, notes, reminders, and step records: until you delete or change them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app
  • Events you exported to another calendar: until you delete them in that calendar
  • Backup files you saved or shared: until you delete them where you saved them. They remain after the app is uninstalled.

b. On our servers and with our service providers

  • Consent records and installation keys: for as long as you use the app and we need to show that consent was given. Deleted when no longer needed, or when you ask us to delete them (§10)
  • Firebase anonymous ID: a random identifier that does not contain your name. When you ask us to delete your information, we delete the sign-in account for this ID together with your consent records (§10)
  • Usage reports: up to 14 months, including the copy we keep for analysis, and then deleted automatically
  • Error reports: up to 90 days, including the copy we keep for analysis, and then deleted automatically
  • Server logs: records of calls to our servers, which may include the anonymous ID, kept for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically
  • Feedback (message, optional email address, optional device information): one year from the day we receive it, then deleted automatically. We delete the copy in our company email inbox on the same schedule. You can ask us to delete it sooner (§10)
  • Spam-protection hash for feedback: it is scheduled to expire about two hours after its time bucket begins; actual deletion may be delayed
  • App integrity, remote settings, and server-call information: for as long as each provider needs it to deliver the service, keep it secure, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements

We cannot remotely delete information that is stored only on your device, events in another calendar service, or backup files you keep.

5. Who we share it with

We do not share your information for purposes unrelated to the app unless the law requires it or you agree. Our service providers act on our instructions or under their own terms, as described here.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Remote Config, Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics): anonymous sign-in, consent records, server features, app integrity checks, remote settings, usage reports, and error reports
  • Google Cloud BigQuery: an analysis copy of usage and error reports, retained for the periods in §4
  • YouTube (Google): if music is offered in the app, playback uses YouTube’s official player. YouTube then receives connection and viewing information under its own terms and privacy policy. YouTube Terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms · Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare R2: if date almanac images are offered, the app downloads the selected image and file list from our image service. Cloudflare receives your IP address, request time, file path and basic connection information to deliver them. The app sends no event, note or step values with these requests. Monthly scenery is bundled in the app and requires no network request. Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Google Workspace (company email): receives a copy of each feedback message, which we delete after one year (§4)
  • Your phone's calendar (such as Google Calendar) and Android activity recognition: used only when you grant permission, to provide calendar import and export and the step features

6. Where information is processed and international transfers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and is processed in these places:

  • Consent records (Cloud Firestore) and our analysis copy (BigQuery): Google Cloud data centres in the United States
  • Feedback (Cloud Firestore): Google Cloud data centres in the United States
  • Server features, including receiving feedback (Cloud Functions): Google Cloud data centres in Seoul, Republic of Korea
  • Company email (Google Workspace), which receives a copy of feedback: Google's global infrastructure, including the United States
  • Other Firebase services: Google's global infrastructure, including the United States
  • Date almanac images, if offered (Cloudflare R2): stored in North America and delivered through Cloudflare’s global network
  • Our team: we operate from the Republic of Korea and may access this information there to support you and run the service

Recipients: Google LLC, Google Cloud and their subprocessors (Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy); Cloudflare, Inc. only if date almanac images are offered (§5); and Google or YouTube only if music is offered (§5).

We operate from the Republic of Korea. Some Google services process information in the United States or other locations outside Singapore. Before such transfers, we use contractual and organisational safeguards intended to provide protection comparable to the PDPA, and we remain responsible for the transferred information as required by the PDPA. You can ask for information about these safeguards at info@connectopia.ai.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications: event reminders and the daily reminder
  • Physical activity: to show your step count and step goal
  • Calendar: to import events from and export events to the calendar on your phone (such as Google Calendar)
  • Alarms and reminders: to show reminders at the exact time you chose
  • Network: to reach our server features
  • Full-screen reminders: to show an important reminder over the lock screen, only if you allow it
  • Ongoing step counting: a notification stays visible while the app counts steps in the background
  • Run after restart: to restore reminders and step counting after your phone restarts

We do not ask for every permission when you first open the app. We ask when you turn on or use the feature that needs it. You can withdraw any permission in your device settings; only that feature is limited.

8. Usage and error reports

The app sends usage reports (which features were used) and error reports (crashes and errors) so we can fix problems and improve the app. They never include your name, your events, your notes, or your step values.

You can turn both off: Settings > Help & backup > Send diagnostic info. Turning it off stops both usage and error reports, and the setting stays off after you restart the app. You can turn it back on at any time, and reports stop being sent from the moment you turn them off. Advertising is disabled for this market.

App integrity checks, remote settings, and server features still handle the basic device and network information described in §2(b) while you use the app.

9. Ads

Advertising is disabled for Singapore in the current app configuration. The app does not load AdMob ads or an advertising consent message for this market. If advertising is introduced, we will update this policy and provide any required privacy choices before it is enabled.

10. Your rights and how to use them

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used (this policy)
  • ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, and for information about how it has been used or disclosed in the past year
  • ask us to correct an error or omission in it
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what happened before. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide a feature that needs it
  • ask us to delete your information; separately, we stop retaining it when it is no longer needed for a legal or business purpose

We do not make decisions about you using automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects.

What you can do yourself

  • On your device: change or delete your profile, events, and settings in the app, or clear the app's data
  • Permissions: withdraw them in your device settings
  • Advertising: disabled for this market
  • Usage and error reports: turn them off or back on in Settings > Help & backup > Send diagnostic info

Information on our servers

Email info@connectopia.ai. Because the app has no sign-up or login, we need a way to find the information that belongs to your app. The easiest way is to open Settings › Help & backup › Terms & Privacy Policy › Privacy request in the app and tap Email us. This creates an email that already includes your app's request code, so we can find your information without asking for anything else. If you write to us another way, please include the request code shown on that screen.

We aim to respond within 30 days. If we need longer, we will tell you when we expect to respond. We may charge a reasonable fee for an access request, and we will tell you the amount before we act.

Feedback you sent: to see, correct or delete a feedback message, email info@connectopia.ai. After you send feedback, the app shows a receipt number. If you email us the receipt number, we can find and delete your feedback. Feedback is not linked to your anonymous ID or your app's request code, so if you no longer have the receipt number, tell us the email address you gave (if any), roughly when you sent it, and a few words from the message.

When we delete your information, we delete the consent records linked to your app's anonymous ID and the sign-in account for that ID. Server logs and usage and error reports are deleted automatically within the periods in §4. We keep a minimal record, linked to the anonymous ID, that shows only when the deletion was made. It contains none of the deleted content and exists so that old consent records sent from your phone are not recreated by mistake. We may keep information when the law requires it.

This contact designation does not state that a statutory appointment has been made. You can also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Singapore: https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email info@connectopia.ai and we will delete it.

12. Other regions

If you change the app's region in Settings (for example, to the United States), the features and privacy policy for that region apply, and the app shows you that region's policy.

13. How we protect information

We limit access, manage permissions, encrypt traffic with HTTPS, keep external keys in protected storage, verify app and device integrity, and limit how often a device may call our servers.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess it and notify the PDPC and affected people where the PDPA requires notification. No method removes every risk. A lost device, a backup file you exported, an operating-system backup, an outage at a service provider, or your network can create risk, so please keep your device locked and your accounts secure.

14. Changes to this policy

If the law, the app's features, or a service provider changes in a way that matters, we will update this policy and tell you in the app or by another appropriate method. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
