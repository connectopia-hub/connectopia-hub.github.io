If you uninstall the app, events already copied into another calendar stay there. You would need to delete them in that calendar app yourself.

The app shows Ireland's public holidays, as published by the Workplace Relations Commission and Citizens Information and set by the Organisation of Working Time Act 1997. Ireland has no substitute day for a public holiday that falls on a weekend, so the app shows the holiday on its own date. It does not show every local, school, or workplace holiday. Holiday dates are included for 2026 to 2028; outside that period the app shows no holiday information.

Dates for 2028 are marked as provisional: they follow the rules in the law, and an official list had not yet been published when the app was released.
