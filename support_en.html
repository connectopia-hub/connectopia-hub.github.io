Common questions

How do I add an event?

  1. Tap a date on the calendar.
  2. Tap Tap to add an event.
  3. Enter a name and time, then save.

I'm not getting reminders.

  • Open Settings › Notifications in the app and make sure reminders are on.
  • Check that notifications for Large Print Calendar are allowed in your phone's settings.
  • On Android, some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. If reminders are late, allow the app to run without battery restrictions.

How do I make the text bigger?

Open Settings › Text size in the app.

How do I move my calendar to a new phone?

Open Settings › Backup & restore in the app. Save a backup file on your old phone, then open it with the app on your new phone.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Your events and notes stay on your phone.
